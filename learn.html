Text description of the video

In this video (about 1 minute 30 seconds), a UW–Madison student housing ambassador named Susy gives an overview of university residence halls. The video shows images and footage of each space and service as it is described.

  • UW–Madison residence halls house over 8,000 students. Living on campus is not required, but over 90% of first-year students choose to.
  • Students live in one of two neighborhoods of roughly equal size: Lakeshore, on the shore of Lake Mendota near Camp Randall Stadium and Union South, or Southeast, near the Kohl Center, Memorial Union, and Bascom Hill.
  • There are over 20 residence halls, including eleven learning communities (faculty-directed programs tied to an academic theme), three theme communities (groups with shared backgrounds or interests), and six dining locations.
  • Every residence hall has common spaces, social lounges, and study dens. Laundry rooms, classrooms, computer labs with printers, group study and tutoring spaces, and hall desks are inside or next to each hall.
  • Each neighborhood has one large dining market, with four smaller locations across campus. All residents choose one of three dining plans.
  • After admission, students receive an email inviting them to log in to My UW Housing and apply. University Housing stays in contact by email throughout the process.

Note: the video does not discuss disability or accessibility features directly. That is part of the activity: as you watch, notice what is shown and what is not.
